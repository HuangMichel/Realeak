Realeak

For fashion brands losing money on products nobody wants, Realeak is an AI agent that reads Vinted every day and flags what's resold brand new, so the next reorder is right.

AttentionThe problem

Brands track returns. They don't see the products customers keep but don't want: those end up on Vinted, brand new.

55.6%
of the 1,094 Cabaïa items for sale on Vinted are brand new (never used) · 28.7% still have their tags
real data, 22 Oct 2026

Why now

  • EU ban on destroying unsold clothing and footwear (ESPR, July 2026): brands must make what people actually keep
  • Second-hand is mainstream: every unwanted product becomes a public listing
  • Vision AI now reads a listing for about $0.001: watching every listing daily is affordable

InterestThe agent

  1. 1Reads Vinted live via Keenable, decides which pages and countries, stops when nothing new
  2. 2Recognizes the product from the photo among 2,038 catalog items, escalates when unsure, says 'unknown' rather than guess
  3. 3Learns: nicknames, confirmed photos and team corrections go to memory
  4. 4Writes recommendations for the product team
