Resale Radar

Vinted is the world's largest panel of disappointed customers. We tell brands which products their customers resell unworn, and why.

The problem

Brands track returns. They don't see the products customers keep but don't want: those end up on Vinted, new, often with tags still on.

The agent

  1. 1Collect: decides which Vinted pages and countries to read through Keenable, stops when new listings dry up
  2. 2Identify: compares each listing photo to 2,038 catalog products (vision), escalates hard cases to a stronger model, says 'unknown' rather than guess
  3. 3Learn: stores aliases and confirmed photos in memory (77 after one run) and human corrections, so the next run is more accurate
  4. 4Explain: per product family, % resold new, resale discount, and written insights for the product team
